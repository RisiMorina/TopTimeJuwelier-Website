## Platform

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS, a self-contained page per version with no build step. The existing v1 (`sites/v1-heritage/index.html`) is already built this way. The site is a small brochure site, and it has to be cheap to host anywhere and easy to hand over to a shop owner who isn't technical. Revisit only if it grows past a few pages.

## Users

The main audience is people who don't know Top Time yet. That means Deventer residents and visitors to the city centre who need a jeweller, a watch battery, a watch repair or a gift, and who might search online or walk past the Spijkerboorsteeg without noticing the shop. Their job is to work out what this shop is, whether it can help them, and how to get there.

The client is the shop owner, who agreed to have a site built but gave no specific requirements. The site is built on spec by a customer, not commissioned by a company.

## Product Purpose

The site is a shop-front website for Top Time Juwelier, an independent jeweller and watch shop in Deventer. It exists to make the shop better known and to bring new people through the door. Success means more walk-ins and phone calls from people who found the shop online.

The site should make plain what the shop is: a physical jeweller where you can walk in without an appointment. It is not a webshop. It sells watches and jewellery and does watch batteries and repairs.

## Positioning

The shop is a long-established independent jeweller in one of Deventer's historic alleys, where you get personal advice and in-house watch service. It sits alongside certified gold and lab-grown diamond collections. A chain store or a webshop can't truthfully claim that mix of place, history, hands-on service and in-person advice. The shop has existed since 1926 (confirmed by the owner), so 2026 is its centenary year. Whether it has always been at the Spijkerboorsteeg is not confirmed.

## Operating Context

- Visitors mostly arrive on a phone, often while nearby or planning a trip into the centre. Their decisions are "is it open, where is it, can they fix my watch".
- Transactions happen only in the shop: no online sales, forms or accounts. The website points to a visit, a phone call or an email.
- Content language is Dutch, for a Dutch-speaking local audience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The shop sells watches and jewellery and does battery replacement (while you wait, with a waterproof test) and watch repair and maintenance.
- The site has no webshop, no forms and no tracking cookies. v1's privacy text relies on this, so any change here also means updating the legal copy.

## Product Principles

1. **Get them through the door.** Every page answers "what is this shop, can it help me, how do I get there, is it open" before anything else.
2. **Real over invented.** Use the shop's own photos, video and verified facts. Never pad with made-up history, reviews or claims; mark anything unverified.
3. **Clear about what it is.** It's a physical shop with walk-ins and no appointment, and it isn't a webshop. Say this plainly so no visitor is confused.
4. **Local and personal.** The shop's edge is place, craft and in-person advice, so lead with those instead of acting like a catalogue.
5. **Easy to hand over.** Keep it simple enough that the owner can host it cheaply and have details like hours and phone numbers updated without a developer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The local walk-in audience includes older visitors, so aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: readable text sizes, strong contrast, large tap targets for phone and route links, and no information conveyed by motion alone. Honour reduced-motion for the hero video.
